OUR STORY

A better kind
of edible.

Well Dosed is being developed in Las Vegas for adults who want an edible experience that feels more considered — from dose to flavor to the ingredients that make the final formula.

We believe the everyday edible can be approachable, intentional and beautifully made.

OUR APPROACH

Intentional Ingredients.

Every ingredient should have a purpose — thoughtfully selected with quality, function and flavor in mind.

Real Fruit Focus

Fruit-forward flavor inspired by ingredients people recognize.

Plant-Based Pectin

A pectin-based gummy concept rather than traditional gelatin.

Bright, Balanced Flavor

Fruit and acid profiles designed for a clean, craveable finish.

Cane Sugar

Sweetness used with intention to support flavor and texture.

Purposeful Formulation

Focused on what earns its place in the final recipe.

Final ingredients and product claims are subject to commercial formulation, testing and regulatory review.
